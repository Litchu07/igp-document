01 — The Problem

The startup ecosystem has an advice surplus and an integration deficit.

There are strategists, finance advisors, growth consultants, operations specialists, and fundraising coaches. For every problem a founder faces, there is a specialist who has seen it before.

The problem is not the absence of expertise. The problem is that these experts work in isolation — and business problems do not. A flawed financial model is often a symptom of an unclear strategy. A broken GTM is often a symptom of misunderstood unit economics.

When founders work with specialists who do not talk to each other, they solve parts of the problem while the whole problem persists. More deliverables. Less progress.

"Founders don't have a strategy problem. They have a business — and a business is a system."

The IGP Thesis

02 — The Philosophy

The business is not the sum of its functions. It is the product of how they interact.

Principle 01
Integration

Business problems do not live in silos. Neither do we. We work across strategy, finance, growth, and operations simultaneously — because that is how real decisions get made. Not five separate workstreams handed to five separate people. One connected system, one integrated partner.

Execution

We do not hand you a deck and leave. Every engagement is measured by what changed in the business — not what was recommended in a report. The work ends when the business is different. That is the only accountability standard we hold ourselves to.

Principle 03

04 — How We Work

Four modes of engagement. Most consultants operate in the first two.

IGP is built for the full sequence — diagnosis through to iteration. The engagement doesn't end at the presentation. It ends when the business is different.

Diagnose

Before we recommend anything, we understand the system — not just the symptom. A structured assessment across strategy, finance, operations, market position, and growth. The full picture, before we touch anything.

Design

We build the integrated plan — strategy and financial model aligned, growth and operations connected. Not five separate plans that will contradict each other in execution. One coherent system everyone can orient around.

Deploy

We work alongside the founder to execute. Building models, frameworks, processes, and systems — staying until they work. This is where most advisory relationships end. It is where IGP does its most important work.

Iterate

Business doesn't stop when the engagement delivers. We build measurement and review into every engagement — so what we build keeps working as the business evolves and assumptions are tested against reality.
